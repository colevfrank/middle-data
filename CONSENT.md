This survey is part of a research study conducted by Cole Frank at Carnegie Mellon University.

Summary  
You are invited to participate in a research study about how people think about sharing personal data with technology companies. You will consider a few short scenarios involving a hypothetical online service and make decisions about whether you would share a specific type of data under different conditions. The study takes approximately 12–15 minutes. Your responses will help inform research on data privacy and governance in the context of AI. You may choose not to participate or to withdraw at any time.
Purpose
The purpose of this research is to understand how people value different types of personal data, and whether the stated purpose of data collection — such as personalization versus training AI systems — changes those valuations. As companies increasingly use personal data to develop generative AI, it is important to understand how people perceive these new uses and whether existing frameworks for thinking about data privacy adequately capture people's concerns.

Procedures  
You will complete an online survey in a single session lasting approximately 12–15 minutes. You will be randomly assigned to consider one specific type of personal data under one set of conditions — not all participants see the same version of the survey. You will be presented with three short scenarios involving a hypothetical online service called "AppX" and asked to make decisions about whether you would share data under different arrangements: a subscription discount, a feature exchange, and a data marketplace. You will then answer questions about your perceptions of the data type, your preferences for compensation models, your use of AI tools, and your general attitudes toward data sharing. The survey concludes with demographic questions.
This study includes comprehension checks to verify that you understand the scenario. If you do not pass the comprehension checks, you may be withdrawn from the study without compensation.

Participant Requirements  
Participation in this study is limited to individuals age 18 and older who are U.S. residents and fluent in English.

Risks
The risks and discomfort associated with participation in this study are no greater than those ordinarily encountered in daily life or during other online activities. You may experience mild discomfort when considering scenarios that involve sharing personal data, comparable to reviewing privacy settings on a consumer technology product. Some questions ask about your attitudes toward AI and technology companies, which some participants may find thought-provoking. All survey responses are collected through an encrypted platform and transmitted securely.

Benefits
There may be no personal benefit from your participation in the study, but the knowledge received may be of value to humanity. Specifically, results may inform policy discussions about data governance, AI regulation, and fair compensation for data contributors.

If you voluntarily withdraw after beginning the survey, partial compensation will be handled in accordance with Prolific's standard policies.

There will be no cost to you if you participate in this study.
